About AfriBite

Connecting people through authentic African flavors and warm hospitality.

Rooted in Tradition, Stirred with Passion

At AfriBite, we are passionate about bringing the rich, vibrant, and diverse flavors of African cuisine directly to your plate. From the sizzling spices of West African Jollof and Suya to the fragrant stewed delights of East and South Africa, our mission is to celebrate African culinary heritage using authentic recipes and fresh ingredients.

Every dish tells a story of family kitchens, festive gatherings, and generations of recipes passed down with love. We are proud to share that story with you, one plate at a time.

Milestones

Our Journey Over Time

2018

The Humble Beginning

Started as a small pop-up food stall serving family-recipe Jollof rice and Suya at local cultural festivals.

2020

First Flagship Location

Opened our flagship dining space in Lagos, offering a warm and immersive culinary experience.

2023

Menu Expansion

Expanded our kitchen offerings to represent iconic recipes from North, East, and South Africa.

2026

Sustainable Sourcing

Partnered with local farmers to ensure 100% organic, locally-sourced African spices and staples.
